<summary>Reflection</summary>

<p>Creating this GIF made me think about how a very simple visual can communicate an idea through repetition. A battery charging is something I see regularly on computers and phones, so the image is immediately familiar. However, turning it into a repeating animation changes how I experience it. Instead of simply showing a battery at one moment, the GIF creates a cycle that keeps starting over.</p>

<p>The color changes are also important to the meaning of the animation. Red represents a low battery, while the colors gradually move through orange and yellow until reaching green at 100 percent. The green battery and the words “FULLY CHARGED!” create a sense of completion, but the GIF does not actually stay completed. It starts charging again. This makes the loop itself an important part of the work.</p>

<p>The simplicity of the GIF was intentional. I wanted to use a familiar digital symbol and change the way it is experienced through animation and repetition. The battery is normally a functional interface element that tells us how much power remains. In this GIF, it becomes something we repeatedly watch, turning a practical symbol into a visual loop.</p>

<p>This project also helped me understand why repetition matters in GIF culture. The animation does not need a complicated story or many visual elements. The repeated movement is enough to create meaning. The viewer watches the battery reach completion and then immediately sees the process begin again, making the idea of an endless charging cycle the main focus.</p>

<p>— Natan Silva</p>
