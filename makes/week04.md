<h2>Panel descriptions</h2>
<ol>
  <li><strong>The Purpose:</strong> A young pilgrim stands in São Paulo with his backpack, looking toward the road. Before starting, he knows his purpose is to strengthen his faith. AI appears on his phone, helping trace the route to Aparecida.</li>
  <li><strong>The Challenges:</strong> The pilgrim walks while AI helps him prepare with route information and possible warnings about weather, traffic, and dangerous areas. The road still brings heat, rain, blisters, long distances, exhaustion, and loneliness.</li>
  <li><strong>The Hard Moments:</strong> Exhausted beside the road, the pilgrim wonders whether he can continue. There are moments when I seriously think about giving up.</li>
  <li><strong>Faith in the Journey:</strong> The pilgrim walks at sunset holding a rosary. Tired and doubtful, I choose to keep going, pray, reflect, and remember why I started.</li>
  <li><strong>The Reward:</strong> After days of walking, the pilgrim sees the Sanctuary of Our Lady of Aparecida in the distance. After every difficult step, I can finally see the destination.</li>
  <li><strong>The Meaning:</strong> The pilgrim kneels inside the Sanctuary before Our Lady of Aparecida. I came with a purpose, faced the challenges, found my faith, and arrived.</li>
</ol>
<p>The journey was never just about walking. It was a journey of faith, strength, and gratitude. Every step, every doubt, and every prayer was worth it.</p>

<details>
<summary>Commentary and reflection</summary>

<p>In this comic, I wanted to show that my pilgrimage to Aparecida was more than a physical journey. Before I started walking, I already had a purpose: I wanted to strengthen my faith. The destination was important, but the meaning of the journey was even more important to me.</p>

<p>I used AI as a tool during the preparation of the pilgrimage. It could help me trace a route, organize the journey, and identify possible difficulties such as weather, traffic, and dangerous areas. However, AI could not walk the road for me or experience the physical and emotional challenges of being a pilgrim.</p>

<p>The comic shows how the journey became difficult through heat, rain, exhaustion, long distances, loneliness, and doubt. At one point, I even thought about giving up. However, prayer and remembering my original purpose helped me continue.</p>

<p>Using the comic format allowed me to show this transformation visually. The first panels focus on preparation and difficulty, while the final panels move toward faith, perseverance, and arrival. In the end, reaching the Sanctuary was not only reaching a destination. It represented the result of continuing even when the journey became difficult.</p>

<p>This experience showed me that AI can help guide the route, but only I can walk the journey.</p>

<p>— Natan Silva</p>
</details>
